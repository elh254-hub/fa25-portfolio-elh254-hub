## About Me 
 
Hello! My name is Emma Harshberger, and I am studying Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University with a technical interest in mechanical design, solid mechanics, materials engineering, thermodynamics, turbomachinery, and high-performance sports equipment. I am especially fascinated by how material behavior, mechanical systems, and thermal-fluid principles drive high-performance hardware and product innovation.

My coursework in dynamics, statics, mechanics of materials, and thermodynamics has strengthened my analytical foundation. Beyond the classroom, I have applied these principles to hands-on projects—such as engineering a cable-suspended perforated filtration system for agricultural harvesting and designing adjustable mechanical assemblies with custom locking-pin mechanisms.

From May 2026 through December 2026, I am completing a Mechanical Engineering Co-Op with Rawlings Sporting Goods, where I conduct dynamic impact testing, process sensor data using MATLAB and DewesoftX, and collaborate with cross-functional teams on prototype development. While I love the intersection of engineering and sports technology, I am eager to explore new industries, gain hands-on hardware experience, and tackle diverse engineering challenges. Balancing this rigorous work with competing as a Division-I athlete on the Cornell Softball Team has honed my discipline, leadership, and ability to execute complex projects under pressure.

My core technical interests include:

Mechanical design and rapid hardware iteration

Solid mechanics, material performance, and structural analysis

Thermodynamics, fluid systems, and turbomachinery

High-performance sports equipment innovation and cross-industry exploration

Looking ahead, I hope to contribute to advanced mechanical innovation, high-performance product development, and rigorous testing environments where hands-on engineering directly shapes reliability and performance.
